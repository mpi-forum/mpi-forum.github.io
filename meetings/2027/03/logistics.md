### Meeting Registration

**Registration is required regardless of whether attending in-person or remote.**

#### Meeting Registration and Voting

**All attendees (in-person or remote)** must register to be eligible to attend
the meeting and vote. It's important to indicate whether you will attend
in-person or remote so we can manage security and logistics.

Registration is required to track attendance, meeting quorum, and voting eligibility.
Please [register here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1bmPLhRaViVl3ZoF3uEN70knW4u1_XXsOVQLKDD_4KLk/viewform).

### Meeting Location

The meeting will be hosted by Meta in New York City, New York, USA.

380 W 33rd St, New York, NY 10001 <br />
[Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/b33UUvTbUpPQAjt98)

You can enter directly from 33rd street, though there isn't signage there to
indicate that it's a Meta office building. Once you are inside, there will be a
registration desk with touch screen devices where you can sign in.
Pre-registration is required and when you arrive, one of the hosts from Meta
will come to the lobby to escort you to our meeting space.

### Travel

Newark, NJ is the simplest airport to get to and from the meeting site. You can
use the airport train system to get to the New Jersey rail system which will
drop you off directly across the street from the office (Penn Station New York,
not New Jersey). Other NYC area airports will also work, but local travel may
be more complex.

### Remote Participation

The meeting times will be during business hours in the local time zone (US
Eastern). Remote participation will also be available via Zoom. The appropriate
links to the meeting will be posted before the meeting to the usual [wiki
page](https://github.com/mpi-forum/mpi-standard/wiki/Virtual-Forum-Meeting-Information).
