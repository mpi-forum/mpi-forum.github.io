## How to use the mailing lists

### Create an account

To subscribe to a list or edit an existing subscription, you need an account
with Argonne National Lab, who graciously hosts our lists for us.
[Sign up for an account](https://mailman.cels.anl.gov/accounts/signup/) (or
[sign in](https://mailman.cels.anl.gov/accounts/login/) if you already have
one).

### Subscribe or unsubscribe

1. Go to the page of the list you want to join (see the table below).
2. Sign in with your account.
3. Use the subscribe/unsubscribe controls on the list page to join or leave
   the list. You can also subscribe without creating an account by using the
   subscription form at the bottom of the list page.

### Manage your subscription

Sign in, then visit the list page to change your subscription settings (such
as delivery options) or to unsubscribe.

### Read the archives

Every list has a public archive of past messages. Follow the "Archives" link
for the list in the table below. No account is needed to read the archives.

### Post to a list

Send email to the list's post address (see the table below). You must be
subscribed to a list to post to it.
